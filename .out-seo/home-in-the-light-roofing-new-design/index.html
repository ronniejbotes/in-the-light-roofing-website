Roofing Contractor FAQs: Answers to Common Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers. From estimates and timelines to inspections and warranties, we make the roofing process easy to understand. Browse our most asked questions or call our team for personalized support today. Hablamos español

What's the difference between free estimates and paid roof inspections?

Free estimates offer a general cost outline for replacement projects. Inspections involve a detailed assessment of your roof’s condition and carry a small fee for the professional evaluation.

Do you offer free Roof Replacement estimates?

Absolutely! Our estimates are specifically designed to give you an idea of the costs involved in replacing your roof. They are provided at no cost to you and offer a detailed breakdown of the expenses associated with your project, helping you plan your budget effectively.

Are your services backed by warranties?

Yes. We provide a 10-year craftsmanship warranty and a 50-year non-prorated materials warranty.

Do you handle storm damage repairs and insurance claims?

Absolutely. We specialize in storm-related roofing repairs and can assist with the entire insurance claim process.

What’s your installation process like?

It includes a consultation call, appointment, estimate, planning, and then production day—handled with complete transparency and care.

Do you provide roof inspections?

Yes, Our roof inspection service goes beyond cost estimation. It involves a comprehensive assessment of your roof’s condition, identifying any existing issues or potential problems. Unlike the free estimates, roof inspections may come with a fee due to the detailed analysis and professional expertise involved. However, they provide invaluable insights into the health of your roof, allowing us to recommend the most suitable solutions for your specific needs.
