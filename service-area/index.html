Bryson’s path to establishing his own roofing company was fueled by a lifelong passion for the roofing industry, supported by unwavering strength drawn from his faith, family, and friends. The foundation of In The Light Roofing was laid with a vision to not just offer roofing services but to encapsulate a sense of belonging and care for each client.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?
Absolutely! We provide no-cost estimates to help you understand your project scope and costs.
Are your services backed by warranties?
Yes, we offer comprehensive warranties, including a 10-year craftsmanship warranty and a 50-year non-pro-rated materials warranty.
Can you handle roof repairs for storm damage?
Certainly. We specialize in storm damage repair and can assist with insurance claims.
What is the process for getting a new roof installed?
Our process is straightforward: initial call, appointment, estimate, pre-production/deposit, and production day.
Do you provide roof inspections?
Yes, we conduct thorough roof inspections to assess the condition and recommend the best course of action.
How can I schedule a service with you?
You can call us at (484) 553-0213 or email us at in**@***************ng.com to schedule a service.
Do you have experience with commercial roofing?
Yes, we have extensive experience in commercial roofing, particularly in EPDM Rubber Roofing.
How does your company ensure customer satisfaction?
We prioritize clear communication, quality craftsmanship, and post-service follow-up to ensure complete customer satisfaction.
