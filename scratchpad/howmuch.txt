How Much Does A New Roof Cost In Pennsylvania?
Having to repair your roof can pose a challenge, especially when you have to replace the entire structure. The whole process can turn into a big inconvenience, and if not properly done, you’ll end up paying more money than you initially thought you would. As problematic as it can be if the situation calls for it, it should be attended to as soon as possible.
Expert Guide to New Roof Cost Estimates
Homeowners will have the option to select different price points for their roof replacement project. Keep in mind, however, lower-cost roofs will actually cost more over time because of the shorter life spans and early development of leaks.
Average roof costs change from state to state across the US, the national average for a new roof is between $8,000 and $15,000. However, the roof replacement cost can vary heavily on many variables, including the size of your roof, materials, and labor cost.
The Average Cost Of A New Roof In Pennsylvania
Materials needed for your roofing project are the biggest factor in any quote. Here are some average material costs per square foot in Pennsylvania.
Materials Average Cost per 100 square foot:
Asphalt Shingles – around $220
Tile Roof – around $970
Wood Shake -around $605
Metal roof – around $860
Slate roof – around $1,260
Flat roof – around $380
How To Know When It’s Time For A Roof Replacement
Sometimes repairing an existing roof is all that’s needed; however, there comes a time when even the best roofing repairs simply won’t cut it. That’s typically the time when you should start considering a roof replacement.
The following factors will help you determine whether or not your home requires a roof replacement.
Water Leaks
One of the more telling signs that your roof requires replacement is when you can notice water damage. Brown stains on your ceiling are a signal that your roof isn’t carrying out its main purpose of protecting your home from the outside elements.
One small water leak can be fixed with a quick repair job, but if a roof is letting in water over several different areas, it’s crucial that you take care of it by having a full roof replacement.
Missing or Damaged Shingles
Missing shingles or damaged areas are other indications that you are in need of a roof replacement. And changing weather can gravely impact the state of your home’s roofing.
Not only can rainy weather damage the roof, but you can also end up missing parts of your roof. So if you notice missing or damaged shingles, it’s definitely time to call a roofing professional.
The roof is Aged
Different types of roofing have different lifespans. For instance, while asphalt shingle roofing only lasts around 20 years, tile roofing can last up to 50 years. Factor in a few damaging storms, and you may need a roof replacement even sooner.
When you’re aware that you have a rather old roof, it’s advised to start thinking about hiring a professional roofing contractor – In The Light Roofing.

What Factors Impact The Average Cost Of A New Roof In Pennsylvania?
Here are some of the variables that influence the new roof cost in Pennsylvania:
Zip code – urban areas often cost more for contractors than rural locations.
Choice of roofing materials.
Whether this is a new roof or an overlay that avoids paying labor for tearing down and dump fees.
The state of the roof deck when it is revealed – do you need any remedial work or repairs?
Roof features – is the design of your roof complex or a simple ranch style?
Are you adding skylights or dormers? This means extra materials and extra labor.
How steep is the pitch? Steeper roof planes always take more work.
What Are Additional Costs For Roof Replacement?
Extra costs will include:
Tearing down and dump fees unless you are opting for a roof overlay.
Scaffolding hire.
New skylights and dormer windows if you are replacing old fittings or adding in new features because you are also converting the roof space.
Costs for an architect or surveyor if you need drawings and plans.
Building permits.
Building inspection fees.
What Other Factors Impact The Choice Of A New Roof?
Budget is definitely one of the main things to consider when picking roofing material. However, one factor to also take into consideration is the general appearance of your house, meaning you are going to pick a roofing material that will go in line with the style of your home.
Most people will first choose materials based on appearance followed by longevity as a new roof is a large outlay, and you certainly want it to last. Somewhere in the mix will be value for money and price, meaning the dream option might just come in over your budget.
What a roof looks like is important in maintaining the value and curb appeal of your home but whether it is weatherproof and watertight is even more important.
Frequently Asked Questions
How Do I Know If My Roof Needs Replacing?
If you are unsure of your roof’s state, then be sure to ask a roofer to inspect your roof for you. Try to find a reputable roofer who isn’t just going to try and sell you a new roof even if you don’t need one.
You can look at your roof from the street, but you won’t be able to see the deck’s condition beneath as the shingles will obscure the view.
Annual roof inspections can provide peace of mind and repair smaller damages that can be easily and affordably remedied, resulting in the prolonged life of the current roof.
What Is A Roof Square?
Roof squares are used by many roofers and roofing materials retailers. One roof square equals 100 square feet. To determine how many roof squares your roof has, you can measure your roof and divide that number by 100, giving you the total number of roof squares.
Roof squares are an easy and convenient way to compare the cost of different roofing materials.
What Is The Best Choice Of Roofing Material For The Climate In Pennsylvania?
The prevailing weather across Pennsylvania can be defined as humid continental. Therefore, picking a roofing material that’s appropriate for this type of weather is crucial.
Asphalt shingles are one of Pennsylvania’s 
most popular roof options
 since they have a long life if the roof is well maintained. They are very easy to fit and work well in the Pennsylvania climate. Additionally, there is a range of designs and pricing options.
Final Words
When it comes to roof replacement in Pennsylvania, you have many options when it comes to materials and aesthetics. You have options for metal, asphalt, wood composite, tile, rubber, and many options for colors and styles in those categories. When you are in a place where you need a roof replacement in Pennsylvania, In the Light Roofing Contracting will be able to work with you and find the best option for your home and your budget.
